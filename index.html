THE FRAME LAB

42.00

A fight measured in frames.

This playable study rebuilds the rhythm, tells, counters, stamina and famous opening-fight trick as a deterministic 60 Hz web game.

01 / TIMING

Console cadence

The simulation advances in fixed 60.0988 Hz steps. The fight clock advances once every 20 frames, preserving the original’s roughly 3× game-time cadence rather than tying combat to display refresh.

02 / INPUT

Six moves, zero mush

Left and right dodge, block, double-tap duck, two hand-specific body blows, hold-up face jabs, and a star-powered uppercut. Inputs are buffered for responsive touch play.

03 / JOE

Vive la frame data

Joe retreats near 0:40. His return has a 16-frame interception window; the opening four frames trigger the special knockout route. Miss it and he gets back up.

04 / METERS

Hearts are not health

Stamina controls knockdowns. Hearts control whether Mac can throw. Blocked or missed attacks drain fighting spirit; clean evasions rebuild it. Stars fuel uppercuts.
